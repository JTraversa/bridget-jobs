9.Federal and federal-adjacent

Tier 2Lane B

Small team, posts rarely, worth a standing alert.

Positioning

Three résumés, not one

The lanes want different framing. A single generic résumé underperforms in all three. Same facts throughout, different order and emphasis.

Lane A · ResearchOps and UXR

Lead with Meta. Reframe the UMD role as research operations: participant pipeline, RA team management, scheduling, tooling, data quality.

Lane B · Applied and survey research

Lead with UMD Smith as experimental behavioral research: design, execution, coding and analysis, plus conference presentation. Master's front and center.

Lane C · Clinical regulatory ops

Lead with TRI: eTMF, document review, regulatory compliance.

Highest leverage

Network, which outperforms all of the above

  • Smith School faculty whose studies you run. They place students into industry and consulting constantly, and they are the warmest referral source available.
  • Meta. A contractor who returns is a much easier hire than a stranger.
  • DC-AAPOR runs local events. For someone in Northern Virginia aiming at survey research, showing up in person is disproportionately effective relative to the effort.
